Heavy Haul Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026. Applies to the Heavy Haul game for Android (package com.heavyhaul.game).

Heavy Haul is a single-player off-road driving game. We built it to collect as little as possible: we do not collect personal data, we show no ads, and we share nothing with advertisers or data brokers.

What is stored, and where

  • On your phone: your game progress (rank, coins, trucks, maps, upgrades, missions), your sound settings and whether you want reminders. This stays on the device.
  • In your Google account, if you sign in to Google Play Games: a copy of the same game progress, saved with Google Play Games "Saved Games", so it comes back when you reinstall or change phones. Google also gives the game your Play Games player id and display name to show who is signed in. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • A random install id: created on your phone and attached to purchases so a purchase can't be copied to another install. It is not linked to your name or email.

Purchases

In-app purchases are made through Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. To confirm a purchase is genuine, the game sends the purchase token Google issued to our verification service, which checks it with Google and keeps no record of you. Google may tell us that a purchase was refunded, and the game then takes back what it gave.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the phone itself schedules a reminder now and then. Nothing is sent from a server and no push token is collected. Turn them off any time in the game's Settings or in Android's settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

  • In the game: Settings > Reset saved game deletes your progress on the phone and in your Google cloud save.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes everything stored on the phone.
  • Your Play Games data can be deleted from your Google account (Play Games app > Settings > Delete Play Games account and data).

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: use the developer contact email shown on Heavy Haul's Google Play page.
