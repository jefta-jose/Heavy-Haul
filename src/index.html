Tune

Touch: GAS / BRAKE pedals. For mud and rock: L, 4×4 and DIFF. Stuck or empty? Call a fuel or breakdown truck from the top bar, back it within 5 m of your truck, tap WINCH to hook on, then drive away to tow it free (tap again to unhook). Keys: → gas, ← brake, W winch on / off, I engine on / off, B handbrake, H / L / R gears, Q 4×4, E diff, V switch vehicle, C camera, Esc pause.
