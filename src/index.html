Sound

Each class of truck has its own recorded engine. Effects are tires, water, mud, brakes and the load; ambience is the birds and the swamp.

How to driveTouch: GAS / BRAKE pedals; the gears shift themselves. Hold BRAKE at a stop to reverse. For mud and rock: 4×4 and DIFF. Stop in a station's bay to refuel and repair. Out of fuel, badly damaged or stuck? Call the breakdown truck from the top bar, back it within 5 m of your truck, tap WINCH to hook on, and tow your truck to a station (tap again to unhook). It comes once per run from the start of the trail; after that its button switches to it. Keys: → gas, ← brake, W winch on / off, I engine on / off, B handbrake, Q 4×4, E diff, V switch vehicle, Esc pause.
