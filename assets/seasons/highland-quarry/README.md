# Slatecut Quarry (autumn)

Status: **playable**

A rainy highland moor around an old slate quarry. For now it drives Copper Ridge's layout
([`../autumn-forest/README.md`](../autumn-forest/README.md)): nine rock fords, seven bogs and its ledges (user decision,
2026-11-17: reuse an existing pattern; a layout of its own can come later).

- **Surfaces:** slate gravel, wet slate (`rock.mu` 0.74), deep peat bogs (`mud.mu` 0.30, `sink` 0.24).
- **Round obstacles:** conveyor rollers (`roller`, the `pipe` art) and timber pit props (`pitProp`, the `logCrossing` art).
- **Low obstacles:** slate scree, rusty cable and gorse heaps, roots over slate, half-buried mining tyres, slate steps, a slate
  pallet and concrete block, and narrow-gauge rails. The slurry ridge and hopper plates were not delivered: scree and rails
  stand in for them.
- **Ambience:** crows and bog frogs; no woodland bed on an open moor.

Art: ChatGPT images (2026-11-17) prepared once into `art/` like Coyote Wash's (see `../desert-wash/README.md`): trimmed
sprites, seamless tiles, the wide strips and the backdrop mirrored to 3:1, the roller and pit prop turned 35° so they run back
into the road. Rock and prop hulls were traced from the trimmed sprites. The entries have no `source`: the encoder skips them.
