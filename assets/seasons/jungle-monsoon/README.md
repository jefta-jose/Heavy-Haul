# Monsoon Canopy (summer)

Status: **playable**

A rainforest in the wet season: the mud map. For now it drives Blackwater Swamp's layout
([`../swamp-summer/README.md`](../swamp-summer/README.md)), the muddiest one with ten bogs (user decision, 2026-11-17:
reuse an existing pattern; a layout of its own can come later).

- **Surfaces:** laterite dirt, wet rock, slick laterite mud (`mud.mu` 0.26, `sink` 0.30).
- **Round obstacles:** huge hardwood logs (`log`, the `logCrossing` art) and bamboo bundles (`bamboo`, the `pipe` art; heavy
  trucks crush them, as they did the swamp's rotten logs).
- **Low obstacles:** palm debris, vine tangles, buttress roots, buried tyres, carved stone steps, broken culverts, river gravel
  and driftwood, overgrown girders and corrugated sheets.
- **Ambience:** the swamp's forest, frogs and crows.

Art: ChatGPT images (2026-11-17) prepared once into `art/` like Coyote Wash's (see `../desert-wash/README.md`); the bamboo
fence and the highway sign came on white and were keyed out. The entries have no `source`: the encoder skips them.
