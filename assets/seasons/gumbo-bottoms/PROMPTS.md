# Gumbo Bottoms: ChatGPT art prompts

The heavy-mud map: a flooded river bottom of black-brown "gumbo" clay, built to test the most powerful trucks.
Start a new ChatGPT chat, paste the **Rules** first, then one prompt per message. Save each image under the file name
shown, in `assets/seasons/gumbo-bottoms/source/`. Say "gumbo-bottoms art is done" when the set is complete.

## Play notes (for integration)

- Almost wall-to-wall mud: ordinary wet mud between bogs, and long `heavyMud` bogs back to back (about 60% of the trail),
  deeper `surfaces.mud.sink` than any other map, and a higher bog drag.
- Firm ground only on short gravel and timber-mat islands, each with a fuel station: a place to breathe and refuel.
- Obstacles sit in the mud: sunken cypress log runs and half-buried culvert pipes, timber mats, root tangles, mud berms.
- A higher trail bonus to pay for the effort. Light scouts will need their breakdown truck; heavy classes with L, AWD and
  DIFF should grind through. This map is meant to be hard, unlike the "keep mud fun" rule on the others.

## Rules (paste first)

> For every image in this chat: you are making art for a 2D side-scrolling off-road truck game. This season is "Gumbo Bottoms", a flooded river bottomland after weeks of rain, where the ground is deep, sticky black-brown clay mud everywhere. Palette: rich chocolate and black-brown mud, olive and moss green, rust orange accents and a bright blue sky with big white clouds breaking up after rain. Style: premium semi-realistic hand-painted game illustration with readable dark contours, weathered materials, glossy wet mud, rich natural texture and soft even daylight, the same style in every image. Objects are drawn in a flat straight-on side view with no perspective; textures are top-down only when I say so. Never draw ice, snow or frost: soft ground is always wet mud. No vehicles unless I ask for a wreck, no people, no animals, and no text, letters, numbers, logos, brands, flags or watermarks unless I give exact wording. Objects go on a real transparent background; if you cannot make one, use a perfectly flat solid magenta #FF00FF background with no gradient, shadow, ground or glow, and no magenta in the object. Show the whole object with a clear margin, nothing touching the image edge. Make each image at the size I give, as large as you can. Generate straight away without asking questions, one image per message. Reply "ready" to this message.
