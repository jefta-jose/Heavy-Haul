How to driveTouch: GAS / BRAKE pedals. For mud and rock: L, 4×4 and DIFF. Empty? Call your class's fuel truck from the top bar and drive it to your truck. Stuck? Call the breakdown truck, back it within 5 m of your truck, tap WINCH to hook on, then drive away to tow it free (tap again to unhook). Each comes once per run from the start of the trail; after that its button switches to it. Keys: → gas, ← brake, W winch on / off, I engine on / off, B handbrake, H / L / R gears, Q 4×4, E diff, V switch vehicle, Esc pause.
